# Hollow Abyss

**Science Fiction · Survival Horror · Exploration**

**Hollow Abyss** is an original tabletop role-playing game developed under **Dice & Vice**.

The game focuses on survival, exploration, limited resources, dangerous environments, and the uncertainty of encountering something that the characters may not fully understand.

Players are placed in hostile situations where preparation, information, equipment, and cooperation can become just as important as direct confrontation.

> Hollow Abyss is currently in active development.  
> Public materials represent only a limited overview of the system and do not contain the complete ruleset or internal playtest documentation.

---

## Overview

Hollow Abyss is built around expeditions into dangerous and unpredictable environments.

Characters may encounter abandoned facilities, unknown phenomena, hostile organisms, damaged infrastructure, isolated survivors, and situations where information is incomplete or unreliable.

The game is intended to create tension not only through combat, but through uncertainty, resource limitations, environmental danger, and difficult decisions.

Survival does not always mean defeating everything encountered.

Sometimes the better solution is to understand, avoid, contain, escape, or simply survive long enough to discover what happened.

---

## Core Experience

Hollow Abyss is designed around several interconnected elements:

**Exploration**

Players investigate locations, search for resources, discover information, and gradually reconstruct what happened around them.

**Survival**

Equipment, supplies, safe locations, and available options may become limited over the course of a scenario.

**Uncertainty**

Players are not expected to immediately understand every threat or phenomenon they encounter.

Observation and information gathering are important parts of play.

**Decision Making**

Many situations are designed to provide several possible approaches rather than a single intended solution.

**Consequences**

Actions taken during an expedition may influence later encounters, available resources, relationships, and the overall condition of the group.

---

## Gameplay Philosophy

Hollow Abyss is not designed purely as a combat-focused system.

Combat is one possible response to danger, but it may also consume resources, attract additional threats, or create new problems.

The system encourages players to evaluate situations before acting.

A successful expedition may involve:

- avoiding unnecessary encounters;
- discovering alternative routes;
- restoring damaged systems;
- negotiating with survivors or factions;
- investigating anomalous phenomena;
- managing limited resources;
- deciding when continuing an expedition is no longer worth the risk.

The intention is to make survival itself part of the gameplay rather than simply a consequence of winning combat encounters.

---

## Inspirations

Hollow Abyss draws inspiration from science-fiction survival and immersive-simulation experiences where the environment itself plays an important role in gameplay.

The project explores ideas associated with isolation, abandoned environments, unknown threats, environmental storytelling, and player-driven problem solving.

These inspirations are used as references for atmosphere and game-design direction rather than as direct adaptations of existing settings.
