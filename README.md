# Blood of Oni

**Dark Fantasy · Japanese Mythology · Political Intrigue**

**Blood of Oni** is an original tabletop role-playing game developed under **Dice & Vice**.

The game takes place in a dark fantasy world inspired by Japanese mythology, where the legacy of the fallen **Hinorami Empire** continues to shape politics, culture, warfare, and the supernatural.

Players become part of a world divided between influential Houses, old traditions, personal ambitions, spirits, yōkai, Oni, and forces that exist beyond ordinary human understanding.

> Blood of Oni is currently in active development.
> Public materials represent only a limited overview of the project and do not contain the complete ruleset, setting documentation, or internal playtest materials.

---

## Overview

The fall of the Hinorami Empire did not bring peace.

Its former territories are divided between powerful Houses, local rulers, military forces, religious traditions, and competing interests seeking to define what should rise from the remains of the old order.

Political conflicts exist alongside supernatural dangers.

Spirits, ancient forces, yōkai, Oni, forgotten traditions, and forbidden practices remain deeply connected to the world and the people living within it.

Characters may become warriors, wanderers, servants of powerful Houses, practitioners of unusual disciplines, investigators of supernatural events, or individuals caught between political ambition and forces far older than human authority.

---

## Core Experience

Blood of Oni is built around several interconnected elements.

### Conflict

Characters may become involved in personal rivalries, political struggles, military conflicts, and supernatural confrontations.

Not every conflict is expected to be solved through direct combat.

### Houses and Allegiances

Power is fragmented between different groups and influential Houses.

Relationships, loyalties, obligations, and rivalries may influence both the story and the opportunities available to characters.

### Martial Disciplines

Combat techniques and martial traditions form an important part of character development.

Characters may gradually develop specialized approaches to combat rather than following a single universal progression path.

### Supernatural Forces

Spirits, yōkai, Oni, rituals, and other supernatural elements exist alongside ordinary political and social structures.

Interactions with these forces may provide opportunities, knowledge, power, or consequences.

### Consequences

Decisions can affect relationships, alliances, reputations, territories, and the position of characters within larger conflicts.

Power often comes with obligations and risks.

## The Fallen Hinorami Empire

The **Hinorami Empire** forms an important part of the history of Blood of Oni.

Although the Empire itself has fallen, its institutions, traditions, conflicts, ruins, political claims, and cultural legacy remain embedded throughout the setting.

Different groups interpret that legacy in different ways.

Some seek restoration.

Others attempt to build something entirely new.

Others benefit from ensuring that the Empire never returns.

The complete history and political structure of the setting remain part of the internal project documentation.

---

## Spirits, Yōkai and Oni

The supernatural is not separate from the world of Blood of Oni.

Spirits and other entities may influence locations, traditions, individuals, and conflicts.

Some are understood and respected.

Others are feared.

Some may be negotiated with, while others represent dangers that cannot be approached through ordinary means.

The nature, classification, and mechanical role of these entities are intentionally not fully documented in the public repository.

## Character Development

Blood of Oni places emphasis on developing a character through choices rather than following a completely predetermined progression.

Development may involve:

- martial disciplines;
- specialized techniques;
- supernatural abilities;
- rituals;
- relationships and allegiances;
- personal development;
- interaction with unusual forces.

The complete progression structure, abilities, balance information, and mechanical requirements remain private during development.

---

## Gameplay Philosophy

Blood of Oni is designed around the interaction between personal stories and larger conflicts.

Characters may be powerful individuals, but they exist within a world shaped by politics, traditions, supernatural forces, and competing ambitions.

A situation may potentially be approached through:

- negotiation;
- political influence;
- investigation;
- alliances;
- ritual practices;
- martial skill;
- deception;
- direct confrontation.

The system is intended to make the consequences of a character's choices matter beyond a single encounter.
