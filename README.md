# Skyfall

**Science Fantasy · Cosmic Survival · Exploration · Dark Adventure**

**Skyfall** is an original tabletop role-playing game developed under
**Dice & Vice**.

The game takes place in a universe approaching the end of its existence.

Ancient civilizations capable of travelling across vast regions of space
and transforming hostile worlds are being forced toward the remaining
survivable regions of the universe.

Empires, factions, survivors, religious movements, and other powers compete
to preserve their people, their history, and their place in whatever future
may still remain.

> Skyfall has a complete playable core and has been tested in actual tabletop
> sessions.
> Development continues through balancing, expansion, refinement, and new content.

---

## Overview

The universe is dying.

Regions that once supported civilizations are disappearing or becoming
increasingly difficult to inhabit.

The surviving powers move toward the remaining areas of stability, bringing
with them centuries of history, rivalries, technologies, beliefs, and unresolved
conflicts.

The result is not unity.

It is competition.

Characters exist inside a world shaped by migration, expeditions, political
struggles, dangerous environments, unusual phenomena, and civilizations
attempting to survive long enough to leave something behind.

## Core Experience

### Exploration

Characters leave relatively stable locations and travel into dangerous or
poorly understood regions.

Exploration may reveal resources, abandoned structures, artifacts, unusual
phenomena, survivors, or threats that cannot be understood immediately.

### Preparation

Expeditions begin before characters enter dangerous territory.

Equipment, information, allies, objectives, and available resources may
significantly influence how a mission develops.

### Survival

Risk is a fundamental part of Skyfall.

Characters are not guaranteed to survive every expedition, and retreat may
sometimes be the most successful possible outcome.

### Consequences

Events occurring during an expedition may continue to influence the characters
after they return.

Losses, discoveries, contracts, relationships, injuries, and other consequences
can shape later sessions.

### Progression

Characters develop through experience, specialization, equipment, abilities,
and the consequences of previous adventures.

Progression is intended to reflect how a character changes through continued
interaction with the world rather than functioning purely as numerical growth.

---

## Expeditions

A major part of Skyfall is built around a recurring expedition structure.

Characters prepare for an objective, leave relative safety, encounter unexpected
situations, and eventually attempt to return with whatever they have gained or
managed to preserve.

A typical cycle may involve:

**Preparation → Expedition → Events → Consequences → Return → Development**

Not every expedition is expected to end according to the original plan.

Players may need to reconsider objectives, conserve resources, rescue allies,
accept new contracts, or abandon a mission entirely.

---

## Conflict

Skyfall supports direct combat, but combat is not intended to exist separately
from the rest of the game.

Enemies and creatures may differ significantly in:

- behavior;
- mobility;
- intelligence;
- resilience;
- anomalous properties;
- weaknesses;
- objectives.

Understanding what a creature wants or how it behaves may be as important as
understanding how much damage it can survive.

---

## Gameplay Philosophy

Skyfall is designed around risk, discovery, and consequences.

Players are encouraged to make decisions with incomplete information and accept
that not every situation has a perfect solution.

A problem may potentially be approached through:

- exploration;
- preparation;
- negotiation;
- environmental interaction;
- resource management;
- cooperation;
- retreat;
- direct confrontation.

The game is intended to create situations where surviving with incomplete
success may be more meaningful than achieving every objective.

---

## A Dying Universe

The approaching end of the universe forms the background of Skyfall.

Civilizations do not simply disappear when confronted with extinction.

They migrate.

They fight.

They cooperate.

They preserve archives, traditions, technologies, religions, and political
institutions.

Others attempt to build entirely new societies.

The surviving regions of the universe therefore become meeting points for
cultures and powers that may never have encountered one another under normal
circumstances.

The deeper causes and larger mysteries surrounding the state of the universe
remain part of the internal setting documentation.

## Current Development

**Status:** Playable / Playtested / Continued Development

The core Skyfall ruleset has been completed and tested in actual tabletop
sessions.

Playtesting has already influenced the development of several systems and
helped identify areas requiring simplification, balancing, or redesign.

The project remains under continued development, with additional work focused on:

- balance;
- content expansion;
- creatures and encounters;
- equipment;
- worldbuilding;
- Game Master tools;
- presentation and documentation.

Individual mechanics may continue to change as additional sessions are played.
